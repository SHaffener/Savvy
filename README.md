## MVP
A user enters or selects a grocery product, compares available prices, identifies the cheaper option, and adds it to a shopping list while keeping track of the budget.

## List of features
1) Compare prices of the same product across a selected number of stores
2) Show the lowest available price and saving amount
3) Add products to a shopping list and display total
4) Automatic receipt scanning
5) Personalized AI recommendations and AI assistance
6) Full grocery catalogs throughout many stores
